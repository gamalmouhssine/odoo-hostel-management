Hostel Management

A complete guest-hostel PMS, built only on Odoo Community

Multi-property rooms and dorm beds, overlap-safe bookings, itemized folios that invoice through standard Odoo Invoicing, housekeeping tasks, QWeb reports, and a set of live front-desk notifications that catch the things a busy front desk would otherwise miss - a deleted folio, a guest who never checked in, a guest who never checked out. No Enterprise modules, no Studio, no third-party dependency: installs and runs identically on Community.

How it works

Set up once, then every stay follows the same path

See it in action

Multi-property master data, all configurable

  • Properties - address, timezone, check-in/check-out times, per-property occupancy rate
  • Room types - private room or dorm, bed type, amenities, photos, a flat default rate
  • Rooms & beds - independent occupancy (state) and housekeeping (housekeeping_status) axes, so a room can be occupied and still dirty at the same time - the two are never conflated into one field
  • Rate plans - price per night, cancellation policy, min/max stay, layered on top of a room type's flat rate rather than replacing it
  • Amenities, bed types, sleeping types, ID document types, cancellation policies, booking sources and charge types are all editable configuration models under Configuration - never a hardcoded picklist you'd need a developer to change
  • Guests live on the standard res.partner (with an ID-document scan, date of birth, and computed stay history) instead of a parallel contact model, so they show up everywhere Odoo already expects a partner

Bookings that can't silently double-book a bed

Book a whole room or a single bed in a shared dorm. A whole-room booking blocks every bed inside that room and vice versa - checked both directions by the same overlap constraint, not two separately-maintained checks that can drift apart.

Pricing is locked at booking time: changing a room type's rate later never moves the price of a booking made under the old rate.

Full stay lifecycle: draft → confirmed → checked_in → checked_out, with cancel and no-show side branches. Check-in correctly cascades room/bed occupancy status, including partial-occupancy dorms (a room only reads "occupied" once every bed in it is).

Deposits, OTA source/reference tracking, and additional-guest lists round out the booking form for real-world front-desk use.

Mechanisms, not just menus

The part that usually gets left for "later" - already built in

  • Live popup notifications for arrivals today, checkouts today, and overstays (a guest still checked in after their checkout date) - reaches anyone with Odoo open, on top of the standard chatter/activity reminders
  • Automatic no-show detection - a confirmed booking nobody checked in by the end of its check-in date is flagged automatically, freeing the room instead of leaving it silently blocked
  • Deposit enforcement - check-in is blocked if a deposit was required and never marked paid
  • Booking confirmation emails sent to the guest automatically on confirmation
  • Housekeeping assignment popups - the moment a cleaning task lands on someone, they know, without checking a list
  • Folio/invoice integrity - cancel or delete an invoice and its folio reopens automatically for re-billing, lines untouched
  • Deletion guards - rooms and beds with real booking history can't be deleted out from under a booking (archiving is offered instead), and a deleted folio doesn't strand its booking - a one-click "Create Folio" recovers it
  • Migration scripts included, so upgrading an existing install carries old data forward cleanly instead of leaving stale values behind

Folios, billing, and housekeeping

Folios open automatically at check-in with a pre-filled stay line, priced off the booking's already-locked rate. Add charges any time - laundry, food, damage, tours, whatever the property needs - then invoice with one click through standard Odoo Invoicing. No custom tax or currency handling: it's a real account.move, so everything downstream (payments, accounting, reporting) just works.

Housekeeping tasks are created automatically on checkout and tracked through pending → in progress → done → verified, with a one-click "Mark Clean" shortcut for the front desk that completes the matching task behind the scenes rather than being a second, disconnected system.

Reports, dashboards, and security

QWeb PDF reports: booking confirmation, check-in registration card, folio, daily arrivals & departures, and an occupancy/ADR summary - plus revenue and occupancy pivot and graph views, and a status-colored Room Kanban board with front-desk saved filters (arrivals today, departures today, in-house now).

Property-scoped security: Staff and Housekeeping roles only see the properties assigned to them (unrestricted if none are assigned, rather than locked out); Managers see everything. Guest ID-document fields are field-level restricted to Staff/Manager only. A separate Housekeeping role sees rooms, beds and tasks - nothing else.

Built and tested against a real Odoo 19 instance

Every mechanism described above is covered by an automated test that exercises the real behavior - not just written and assumed correct. No Gantt view, no Studio dependency, no Documents app dependency, no POS or portal integration: deliberately scoped to what a standalone hostel front desk actually needs.
